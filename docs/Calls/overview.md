# Overview

**WhatsApp Calls** allows you to stay connected with your contacts anytime and anywhere. You can make voice and video calls over WiFi or mobile data.
This feature offers several options to help you communicate better. You can make instant calls, schedule calls for later, view call history, and add favorite contacts for calls management.

> **Information!**
> Every call uses an end-to-end encryption to keep your conversations private and secure.

**Key Features**

* Make voice and video calls instantly.

* Schedule calls in advance with your contacts.

* View complete call history with dates and times.

* Add favorite contacts for quick access.

* Receive reminders before scheduled calls

* Share scheduled calls with your calendar.

**Before You Begin**

Before you start using WhatsApp Calls, ensure the following:

- You have an active internet connection (WiFi or mobile data).

- Your contacts have WhatsApp installed on their devices.

- Your device microphone works properly.

- (For video calls) Your device camera works properly.
