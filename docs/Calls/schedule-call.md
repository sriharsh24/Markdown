# Schedule a call

This topic helps you schedule a WhatsApp call to set a call time with your contact. Your contact receives the scheduled call notification with date and time.

To schedule a WhatsApp call,

1. Open WhatsApp and tap the **Calls** tab.
   
   The **Calls** screen appears.

   ![Calls Screen](/images/calls-screen.png)

2. Tap the **Schedule** icon (calendar icon).
   
   The **Schedule Call** screen appears.

   ![Schedule call screen](/images/schedule-call-screen.png)

3. Enter a call title in the **Call name** field.

4. (Optional) Enter a description for the call.

5. In the **Starts** field, select the date and time.

6. In the **Ends** field, select the end date and time.

7. (Optional) Tap the toggle button to include end time to add duration.

8. Select the call type:
   
   - Voice

   - Video

9.  (Optional) Tap the **Require approval to join** toggle button to accept the attendee to your call.

10.	Select the reminder time from the **Reminder** dropdown. You can select any one of the options:
    
    - 15 minutes before
    - 30 minutes before
    - 1 hour before
    - 1 day before
    - Never

11. Tap **Next**.
    
    The **Send to screen** appears.

12. Select a contact or group.

13. (Optional) Tap **Add to calendar** to save the meeting details to your calendar.

14. Tap **Next** from the bottom right corner of the screen to send the scheduled call.

Your contact receives the scheduled call notification. The notification displays the call title, date, time, and call type.
